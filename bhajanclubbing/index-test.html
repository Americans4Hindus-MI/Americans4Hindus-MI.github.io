WELCOME TO OUR COMMUNITY

Welcome to the Uttar Pradesh Association of Detroit

UPAD brings together families and friends with connections to Uttar Pradesh and a shared commitment to our culture, traditions, and community here in Michigan. Through festivals, cultural programs, family gatherings, and volunteering, we build friendships that span generations.

America is our Karmabhoomi—the country we call home and to which we contribute. As Indian Americans, we believe in integration, not assimilation or isolation: participating fully in American life while preserving our heritage and passing it proudly to our children and grandchildren.

Learn more about UPAD and our vision ＋

We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, supported by members and volunteers. Our goal is to become an even more member-driven community: listening to what members want, creating meaningful programs, and offering more member-focused activities and events.

We want our children to know their roots, our grandchildren to inherit living traditions, and every member to feel they have a voice. That takes more than an organizing committee. It takes your ideas, participation, talents, and enthusiasm. Together, we can build a UPAD that future generations will be proud to carry forward.

Stay connected through our UPAD website. Links to our official WhatsApp community and Facebook groups will be added after confirmation.
