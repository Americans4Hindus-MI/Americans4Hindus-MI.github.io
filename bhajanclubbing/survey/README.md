
# A4H Bhajan Clubbing 2026 – Attendee Feedback Survey

## Purpose
Official post-event feedback survey for A4H Bhajan Clubbing,
held on October 23, 2026, in Southfield, Michigan.

The survey collects attendee feedback to help Americans
for Hindus (A4H) improve future events, strengthen community
engagement, and encourage continued participation.

## Public Survey URL
https://americans4hindus-mi.github.io/bhajanclubbing/survey/

## Website
- Hosted on GitHub Pages
- A4H navy-blue and gold branding
- Mobile-friendly HTML survey
- No Google Forms dependency

## Survey Fields
1. Full Name (required)
2. Email Address (required)
3. Phone Number (required)
4. Overall Event Rating – 1 to 5 stars (required)
5. What Did You Enjoy Most? (required)
6. Suggestions for Future Events (optional)
7. Consent to Receive A4H Updates (optional)

## Backend
- Google Apps Script Web App
- Runs under the A4H CIO Google Workspace account
- Responses stored in a private Google Sheet
- Spreadsheet: A4H Bhajan Clubbing 2026 – Attendee Feedback
- Worksheet: Survey Responses
