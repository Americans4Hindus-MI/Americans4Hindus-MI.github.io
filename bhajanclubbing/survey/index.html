A Little About Americans4Hindus

For those discovering A4H for the first time, we are a community-driven organization working to strengthen the voice of Hindu Americans.

We have built the American Dream. Now we are working together to protect it for future generations.

From temple safety and religious freedom to fair representation of Hinduism in education, A4H engages community leaders and lawmakers on issues that matter to our families.
