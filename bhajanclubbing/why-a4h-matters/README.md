# Why A4H Matters to Our Families

A concise, approximately one-minute introduction to Americans4Hindus (A4H) for Bhajan Clubbing attendees and their families.

## Public page

https://americans4hindus-mi.github.io/bhajanclubbing/why-a4h-matters/

## Purpose

Introduce A4H's community priorities in a welcoming, accessible way: temple safety, religious freedom, fair representation of Hinduism in education, and civic participation. Readers who want more detail can explore the Additional Resources links.

## Deployment

This folder is located at `bhajanclubbing/why-a4h-matters/` in the `Americans4Hindus-MI.github.io` repository. The public page must be saved as `index.html` in this folder. The versioned source file is `a4h_why_it_matters_index_v2.html`.
