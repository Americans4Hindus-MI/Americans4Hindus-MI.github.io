Why A4H Matters to Our Families

We have built the American Dream. Now let's build the collective voice to protect it for future generations.

Our nationality, heritage, and Dharma are complementary parts of our identity.

Our families contribute to American life in countless ways. Yet issues affecting our temples, our faith, and our children's understanding of their heritage deserve greater attention.

What matters to our families?

Safe temples

Places of worship protected from threats, vandalism, and hate.

Religious freedom

The ability to practice and celebrate our faith without discrimination.

Our children's education

Accurate, balanced teaching about Hinduism and its contributions.

A voice in public decisions

Our concerns heard by lawmakers, school boards, and community leaders.

What does A4H do?

Americans4Hindus brings people together to encourage civic participation, engage elected officials, and advocate for these priorities through constructive dialogue.

Our Hope: That our children and grandchildren grow up proud to be American, proud to be Indian American, and proud to be Hindu American.
American by nationality. Indian American by heritage. Hindu American by Dharma.
